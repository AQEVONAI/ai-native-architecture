# How Mature Is Your AI-Native Architecture, Really?

Most maturity assessments collapse into a single score — which sounds reassuring and hides exactly the thing that matters most. An organization can be genuinely strong on knowledge grounding while having no real enforcement around what its agents are allowed to do. Averaging those two realities into one number erases the imbalance that's most likely to cause an actual incident.

## How we assess it instead

We score six domains independently — Knowledge, Intelligence, Autonomy, Control, Operations, and Evolution — each on a five-level scale from Ad Hoc to Optimizing. No domain's strength offsets another's weakness in the final picture, because in practice, it doesn't offset it in reality either.

We also insist on evidence, not self-report. "We do grounding" isn't an answer we accept without seeing a specific example traced end to end. "We have human review" isn't credited without checking the actual approval rate — because a 99% rubber-stamp rate isn't a safety control, no matter what the process documentation says.

## What comes out of it

Not a badge. A prioritized, sequenced roadmap — specific gaps, specific patterns that close them, and an explicit rule for what to fix first (hint: an autonomy level that's outpaced your enforcement maturity is usually the most urgent gap, even if it's not your lowest score).
